# TrackZero launch film (Phase 40)

An 88.8 s film (37 bars at 100 BPM) in two cuts: 1920 × 1080 and a vertical 1080 × 1920. It shows the real app:
every phone screen is a screen recording of the built app on the Pixel 7 emulator, or one of the app's own screenshots
(`shots/`). Everything else (type, the mark, motion, music and sound) is generated by the scripts here, so the project
is fully editable and has no third-party media.

## Files

- `record.sh` — drives the app on the emulator and records each scene (`adb screenrecord`, 1080 × 2400).
- `film.py` — the timeline (`SCENES`: bars, the recording's keyframes, touches, words), the layout for both
  aspects, the compositor (numpy + OpenCV + Cairo + Pillow) and the encoder (ffmpeg, H.264 CRF 16, AAC 256 kb/s,
  loudness normalised to −14 LUFS / −1.5 dBTP).
- `sound.py` — the score and the sound design (`CUES`), synthesised with numpy.

## Rebuilding

1. Emulator, debug build, Puy-de-Dôme map, the "Aubière - Clermont-Ferrand (6.6km)" route imported, tutorial done.
2. For clean footage: build once with the engine's debug label off (in `libs/drape_frontend/frontend_renderer.cpp`,
   the `SetVisible(true)` under `#if defined(BUILD_DESIGNER) || defined(DEBUG) ...` disabled; do not commit it), and
   put SystemUI in demo mode (clock 09:41, full battery, no notification icons):
   `adb shell settings put global sysui_demo_allowed 1` then `adb shell am broadcast -a com.android.systemui.demo -e
   command enter` (and `clock -e hhmm 0941`, `battery -e level 100 -e plugged false`, `notifications -e visible
   false`). Exit with `-e command exit` afterwards.
3. `tools/trackzero/film/record.sh <raw dir>` (all scenes, about 12 minutes; or name scenes). The emulator drops
   some swipes: look at each recording; re-record a scene by name.
4. `python3 tools/trackzero/film/film.py --raw <raw dir> --out film.mp4` and `--aspect 9x16` for the vertical cut.
   `--scale 0.5` renders a quick preview; `--still <s> --out frame.png` renders one frame.
5. If a re-recorded scene's moments move, adjust its `keys` and `taps` in `film.py` and its `CUES` in `sound.py`
   (all in seconds into the scene).
